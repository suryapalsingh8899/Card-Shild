METHODOLOGY

Analysis Architecture

Seven behavioral dimensions evaluated sequentially to produce a unified risk index

01 / VALIDATION

Card Status

Verification of card status, expiration dates, and account standing. Blocked credentials trigger instant critical alerts.

02 / MAGNITUDE

Amount Variance

Evaluates amount deviations against historical transaction means and cardholder credit utilization limits.

03 / TEMPORAL

Chronological Pattern

Flags non-standard hours, midnight ATM attempts, and off-schedule point-of-sale activities.

04 / GEOLOCATION

Cross-Border Scrutiny

Heightened behavioral analysis for international merchants, cross-border terminals, and foreign conversions.

05 / CATEGORY

Merchant Profiling

Cross-references transaction category with historical fraud density across 3M benchmark bank transactions.

06 / VELOCITY

Frequency Spike

Detects rapid repetitive transactions within 24 hours, characteristic of automated card-testing bots.

07 / SYNTHESIS

Cost-Sensitive Ensemble AI (Accuracy Paradox Solved)

Due to severe class imbalance (0.5% fraud base rate), a naive model yields a deceptive 99.5% accuracy while missing 100% of frauds. CardShield resolves the Accuracy Paradox using cost-sensitive class balancing and multi-dimensional behavioral heuristics to accurately isolate rare fraud vectors.
